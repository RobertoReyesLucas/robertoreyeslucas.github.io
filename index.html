About me

I was born and raised in Málaga, on the south coast of Spain. That's where I studied Marketing and Market Research at the University of Málaga, and where I first learned that good marketing starts with understanding people and the numbers they leave behind.

Wanting to grow further, I moved to Ireland to take an MSc in Digital Marketing at Maynooth University. Studying and living in a second language and a new country has made me more adaptable, more independent and more confident working with people from different backgrounds. I work fluently in both Spanish and English (C1).

My interest in technology comes from curiosity and a dislike of wasted effort. I like testing new tools, finding the bottleneck in a process and building the automation that removes it. Every workflow I automate frees up time for the strategic and creative work that actually moves results, and every new tool I learn opens another way to grow.

I treat learning as part of the job. I'm usually studying something new alongside my work, whether that's a new ad platform feature, an AI model or a better way to structure a website, because this field changes every few months and I want to be the person on the team who's already tried it.

Outside work I stay active with sports and I'm rarely without music. Both keep me focused and give me the energy I bring to my projects.
